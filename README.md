# anaar-experiments
## Web Slinger

`web-slinger/index.html` is a 3D web-swinging game inspired by the PS5 Spider-Man games, in one self-contained HTML file (Three.js r128 from cdnjs). Open it in a browser to play.

- Hold swing and webs attach to real building edges ahead of you, then chain automatically down the street canyons.
- Tap jump just past the low point of a swing for a timed swing-jump boost. In the air, jump web-zips you forward.
- Steer into a wall to wall-run it. Zip to the spot under the centre dot, and tap jump as you reach a ledge to point-launch.
- Dive to build speed for the next swing. Collect 40 gold tokens, and a marker points to the nearest one.
- Plays with mouse and keyboard, a gamepad, or touch (floating stick and buttons). Physics runs at a fixed 120 Hz, so it behaves the same at any frame rate, and render quality adapts to the device.
